## Course Content

**1. Introduction to measurement** \
Measurement; measuring instrument; transducers & sensors; generalized static characteristics of sensors; generalized dynamic characteristics; error analysis; sampling.

**2. Basic instrumentation** \
Operational amplifiers; linear circuits with op-amps; first- and second-order filters; system identification.

**3. Measuring movement: linear and rotational** \
Resistive; inductive; capacitive; digital sensors; camera-based tracking; accelerometers; gyroscopes.

**4. Measuring effort: force and torque** \
Strain gauges.

**5. Measuring pressure, flow, and volume** \
Piezoelectric sensors; differential/absolute pressure, flow, and volume sensing methods.

**6. Measuring temperature** \
Thermocouples; thermistors; radiation thermometry.

**7. Measuring biopotentials** \
Origins of biopotentials; review of basic electrochemistry; electrode half-potentials; electrical equivalent circuits of electrodes.

**8. Measuring chemicals** \
pH sensors; oxygen and carbon dioxide sensors; glucose sensors.
