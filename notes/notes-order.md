# Order of the companion notes

The reading order of the notes pages. Each generator reads this table and puts a
previous/next bar at the end of its page, and `build-index.py` makes
`notes/index.html` from it. To insert a note, add a row; to reorder, move rows.
Paths are relative to `notes/`. Keep the table's three columns and one note per row.

| # | File | Title |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | 01-kinematics-kinetics/ik-notes-thigh.html | Inverse kinematics of a single link |
| 2 | 01-kinematics-kinetics/ik-notes-leg.html | Inverse kinematics of a two-link leg |
| 3 | 01-kinematics-kinetics/fiducial-notes.html | Pose of a fiducial marker from one image |
